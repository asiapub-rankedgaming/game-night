Asia Pub: Game Night

Player Details & Event Information

01

What is it?

A monthly Game Night for players in the Asia Public channel.

Come play during the official event hours, compete with other active players, and fight for the top spots.

02

When?

4th Week of Every Month

🕖 19:00 RMT – 22:00 RMT

The exact event date will be announced in the Asia Public channel.

03

How Does It Work?

  • Play during the official Game Night schedule.
  • Your performance during the event period will determine the standings.
  • Overall ladder/MMR is NOT the basis of the event.
  • Only verified games played during the official schedule will count.
04

To Have Your Game Counted

Your game must be completed during the official Game Night coverage.

After playing, submit:

1. Your Ladder Link
2. Game ID

That's it. Staff will handle the verification and recording.
